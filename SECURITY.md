# Security Policy

SyntaxRoot is a product of Thought Parameters LLC. This policy covers the SyntaxRoot service at [syntaxroot.cc](https://syntaxroot.cc) and every repository in the [syntaxroot-cc](https://github.com/syntaxroot-cc) organization unless a repository defines its own.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report vulnerabilities privately through either channel:

- GitHub: use **"Report a vulnerability"** on the repository's **Security** tab (private vulnerability reporting)
- Email: **hello@syntaxroot.cc** with the subject line `SECURITY`

Include the affected component (web app, API, or repository), steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed. Please don't access or modify other customers' data while testing.

## What to Expect

- We acknowledge new reports within **3 business days**.
- We send an initial assessment and planned next steps within **10 business days**.
- We coordinate a disclosure date with you and credit you, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: syntaxroot.cc, api.syntaxroot.cc, and code in public syntaxroot-cc repositories. Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering, and third-party services we don't control.
